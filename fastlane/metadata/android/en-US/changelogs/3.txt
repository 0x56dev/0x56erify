0.1.2 - Build/release metadata fix

- Disable Android Gradle Plugin's dependency metadata block
  (dependenciesInfo.includeInApk/includeInBundle) in the release build.
  This block is used for Play Console reporting and is irrelevant off
  Play; it was flagged by F-Droid's reproducible-build tooling as an
  unwanted extra signing block. No app functionality changed.
