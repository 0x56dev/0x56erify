0.1.3 - Bug fix

- Fix pasting several checksum lines (e.g. a whole SHA256SUMS file) into
  the expected-hash field: only the first hash was used, so a genuine file
  listed on a later line showed MISMATCH. Input with more than one
  different hash, or with lines that are not checksum lines, is now
  rejected with an explanation instead of being guessed at.
